## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional, yet technologically advanced foundation for national grievance redressal, public accountability, and real-time civic operational monitoring. It blends the structural rigor of sovereign governance platforms with the precision, velocity, and visual clarity of mission-critical SaaS enterprise infrastructure.

The aesthetic follows an **Enterprise Civic Precision** archetype:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, transparent, resilient, calm under pressure, and radically legible.
- **Audience:** High-level departmental secretaries, regional ombudsmen, field grievance redressal officers, and data intelligence analysts processing high-velocity civic telemetry.
- **Visual Stance:** A structured combination of deep sovereign slate navigation, bright analytical white surfaces, hairline borders, and tactical micro-indicators. It avoids frivolous decoration, focusing on fast scannability, deterministic status classification, and high-density situational awareness.

## Colors

The palette employs a high-contrast dual-tone architecture: deep, formal slate navy foundations for navigational anchoring, juxtaposed against clean, cool-tinted analytical working surfaces designed for long-shift operational comfort.

### Functional Application Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` provides a neutral, anti-glare operational field. Sectional groupings and tertiary wells utilize `#F1F5F9`.
- **Primary Surfaces:** Pure `#FFFFFF` cards, data tables, and modal layers framed by hairline boundaries (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Sidebar & Navigational Chrome:** High-authority sovereign dark canvas (`#0A0F1D` base, `#0F172A` structural chrome, `#1E293B` panel cards) bounded by precise architectural borders (`#334155`).
- **Interactive Accents:** Civic Blue (`#2563EB`) commands all primary actions, active indicators, and focal entry points; Departmental Indigo (`#4F46E5`) is reserved for cross-departmental coordination pathways.

### Telemetry & Redressal Status Tokens
- **Resolved / Compliant:** Emerald (`#10B981`) text/fill anchored over `#ECFDF5` container surfaces with a `#A7F3D0` subtle border.
- **In-Progress / Routine Triage:** Civic Blue (`#3B82F6`) on `#EFF6FF` containers with `#BFDBFE` structural accents.
- **Pending / Action Window Aging:** Amber (`#F59E0B`) on `#FFFBEB` containers with `#FDE68A` warning lines.
- **Overdue / SLA Breach / Critical Escalation:** Crimson (`#EF4444`) on `#FEF2F2` containers with `#FECACA` alert perimeters.
- **AI Synthesis & Intelligent Briefing:** Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) foreground on `#EDE9FE` surfaces with `#DDD6FE` framing.

## Typography

Typography enforces uncompromising visual structure. It pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers, analytical KPI displays, and section anchors with **Inter** for transactional body text, audit logs, and administrative controls.

### Implementation Rules
- **Tabular Numerics:** Enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` across all analytical metrics, SLA countdowns, timestamp logs, grievance tracking IDs, and table cells to prevent layout jitter across live updates.
- **Categorical Micro-Badges:** All label tokens configured as `label-sm` must carry uppercase transformation (`text-transform: uppercase`) and wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em`) to ensure instant recognition within high-density lists.
- **Hierarchy Enforcement:** Section titles never exceed `headline-md` within cards. Data telemetry headers prioritize numerical value dominance (`metric-display`) with secondary tracking badges placed alongside or immediately underneath.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8-point harmonic rhythm integrated with an enterprise 12-column responsive grid architecture.

### Grid & Layout Geometry
- **Global Shell:** A persistent 280px deep-navy navigation bar docked to the left, paired with an optional 360px contextual sliding triage drawer on the right.
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Analytical cards map strictly to 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 column modules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`. Side navigation collapses into an iconified 72px rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `gutter-sm: 0.75rem` and `margin-sm: 1rem`. Global navigation converts into an overlay drawer.

### Data-Density Strategy
Cards use compact internal padding (`space-md` or `space-lg`) to balance screen real estate across enterprise monitors without sacrificing visual air around complex citizen grievance records.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a crisp hybrid of **hairline structural borders** and **ambient cool-tinted shadows**, steering clear of heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`, flat, zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Operational Cards, Grids, and Rows):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, underpinned by an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdown Menus, Contextual Flyouts):** Pure white `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, elevated with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Citizen Detail Drawers, Escalation Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #94A3B8`, elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Sidebar Elements (Dark Inversion):** Deep navy `#0F172A` with `#1E293B` surfaces framed by `#334155` outlines; zero shadow, maintaining planar structural authority.

## Shapes

The design system uses a calibrated rounded geometry (Level 2: Rounded). This softens dense analytical data without compromising its institutional identity.

### Structural Radius Mapping
- **Primary Cards, Analytics Panels, and Data Surfaces:** Configured to `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) for an elevated SaaS aesthetic.
- **Major Modal Containers, Citizen Profile Drawers, and AI Briefing Banners:** Configured to `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Text Inputs, Segmented Switchers, Select Fields):** Configured to `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Status Badges, SLA Timers, and Metric Chips:** Configured to `rounded-full` (9999px) to maintain visual separation between interactive targets and passive indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#2563EB` fill, white typography (`label-lg` or `label-md`), `0.5rem` radius, with subtle hover transition to `#1D4ED8`. Active state deepens to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary (Institutional):** Crisp white surface with `#E2E8F0` border and `#0F172A` text. Hover adds a `#F8FAFC` background tint and `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Destructive Escalation:** `#EF4444` solid with white text, transitioning to `#DC2626` on hover. Used exclusively for critical SLA breaches, manual overrides, and grievance rejections.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#64748B` text, transitioning to `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Grievance Status Chips & Metric Badges
- Built using full pill radii (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical by `space-sm` horizontal padding, paired with `label-sm` uppercase typography.
- Always include an absolute-centered `6px` status indicator dot on the leading edge (pulsing on Overdue/Escalation tiers).
- Colors rely strictly on the semantic pairings defined in the color specification (e.g., `#ECFDF5` container with `#065F46` label).

### Data Tables & Grievance Lists
- Rows have a minimum height of `52px` with a subtle `#F1F5F9` bottom dividing line.
- Header row: `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase `11px` tracking with `#64748B` label text.
- Row states: Default `#FFFFFF`, hover `#F8FAFC`, active/selected `#EFF6FF` with a left `#2563EB` 3px accent bar.
- Tabular numeric alignment: Right-align monetary allocations, SLA hours, and telemetry percentages; left-align grievance ticket IDs and subject narratives.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Base Input:** 40px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `0.5rem` radius, `Inter` 14px text.
- **Focus State:** `1px solid #2563EB` surrounded by an outer focus ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Search & Omnibox:** Prepended by a search icon in `#94A3B8`, with an inline monospaced hotkey indicator (`⌘K`) anchored to the trailing edge.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes feature a `4px` radius, styled with a `#CBD5E1` border. Checked state displays `#2563EB` with a crisp white check icon.
- Radios feature standard circular form factor, with an active `#2563EB` outer boundary housing a centered `#2563EB` dot.

### Specialized Civic SaaS Components
- **AI Intelligent Briefing Tile:** Backed by an `#EDE9FE` container, bordered with `#DDD6FE`, using a `#7C3AED` header and `#4F46E5` icons to clearly demarcate machine-generated insights from human audit logs.
- **SLA Countdown Timer:** High-visibility monospaced module (`data-mono`) featuring a two-state transition: calm `#64748B` when within threshold, changing to `#EF4444` background text with a `#FEF2F2` pill when the grievance enters critical escalation (under 4 hours).
